# humanities-academic-paper-reading-studying

A Codex/ChatGPT skill for deep academic reading in the humanities and social sciences.

This skill turns the assistant into an academic reading tutor for scholarly papers, book chapters, course readings, and groups of related texts. It is designed for close reading rather than simple summarization: the assistant helps identify a text’s research context, core problem, thesis, argument structure, theoretical framework, methods, contribution, limitations, and relation to broader scholarly debates.

## What It Does

This skill helps with:

- Reading and analyzing academic papers, articles, essays, and book chapters
- Explaining difficult passages, concepts, and theoretical terms
- Reconstructing arguments step by step
- Formalizing dense philosophical or theoretical arguments when appropriate
- Identifying research questions, methods, contributions, and limitations
- Comparing multiple texts on the same topic
- Synthesizing literature for seminar preparation, research notes, or literature reviews
- Explaining humanities and social-science texts in a clear, structured, source-faithful way

## Fields Covered

The skill is especially suited for:

- Philosophy
- Religious studies
- Literary studies
- Linguistics
- History
- Sociology
- Anthropology
- Cultural studies
- Communication
- Political theory
- Gender studies
- Postcolonial studies
- Art history and visual culture
- Other text-, theory-, concept-, history-, or society-centered disciplines

## Core Principles

The skill emphasizes:

- Faithfulness to the supplied text
- Clear distinction between the author’s claims and background context
- No invented quotations, page numbers, or arguments
- Short, purposeful quotation when useful
- Explanation of why a text matters academically
- Attention to argument, method, theory, and scholarly conversation
- Constructive critical evaluation grounded in the source
